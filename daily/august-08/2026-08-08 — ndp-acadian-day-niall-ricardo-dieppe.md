## Celebrate Acadian Day with President Niall Ricardo

Saturday, August 15th, 10:00am (Atlantic time)

Join New Democrats from across New Brunswick for a morning of organizing, learning, and connection as we continue to strengthen our federal movement.

Whether you're a long-time volunteer, a newly signed-up member, or someone interested in helping build a stronger Federal NDP in your community, this event is designed to give you the tools, knowledge, and connections to get involved.

We are pleased to welcome Federal NDP President Niall Ricardo as our special guest speaker, who will share updates on the party, the importance of grassroots organizing, and the opportunities ahead for members across the country.

*The morning will include:*

- A casual meet-and-greet with Federal President Niall Ricardo
- A New Member Orientation for anyone looking to better understand the party and ways to get involved
- A practical workshop on activating and strengthening your local Federal Electoral District Association (EDA)

Whether you're looking to reconnect with your local EDA, help build one where none currently exists, or simply meet fellow New Democrats, we hope you'll join us for this exciting morning of movement-building.

After the event, we invite everyone to continue the day together by joining us at Dieppe's Acadian Day celebrations and parade. It's a wonderful opportunity to celebrate Acadian culture, connect with the community, and enjoy one of New Brunswick's most vibrant annual traditions.

- Saturday August 15th
	10:00am - 12:00pm (Atlantic time)
- Dieppe Arts & Culture Centre
	331 Acadie Ave, Dieppe, NB
	Multifunctional Room
