CPAC coverage of a House of Commons foyer media availability in which NDP leader Avi Lewis, joined by Grassy Narrows First Nation Chief Sherry Ackabee, community member Chrissy Isaacs, and NDP MP Leah Gazan, pressed the Carney government for federal action on mercury contamination affecting the community. The availability reiterated calls for the prime minister to apologize for his "outlast" remark and to deliver measures addressing the ongoing environmental harms at Grassy Narrows.
