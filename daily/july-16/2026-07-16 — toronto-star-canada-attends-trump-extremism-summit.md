The Toronto Star reports that the Trump administration is urging the Carney government and other allies to treat left-wing extremism as a top national security threat, a shift in counterterrorism strategy raising concerns about civil liberties and its potential impact on Canada. Secretary of State Marco Rubio and White House adviser Stephen Miller laid out the priority Thursday before a senior Canadian embassy official and representatives from more than 60 countries, including the full G7, at the Washington summit. Federal NDP Leader Avi Lewis condemned Canada's participation as a "new McCarthyism" — see the accompanying NDP statement and Western Standard coverage from the same day.*
