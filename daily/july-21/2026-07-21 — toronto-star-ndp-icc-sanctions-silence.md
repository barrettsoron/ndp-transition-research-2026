NDP foreign policy critic Heather McPherson says the Carney government's support for international law rings hollow because it isn't calling out Washington over its attacks on the International Criminal Court, including sanctions on Winnipeg-born ICC judge Kimberly Prost. Foreign Affairs Minister Anita Anand says Canada supports international law and the global court should never be politicized.
