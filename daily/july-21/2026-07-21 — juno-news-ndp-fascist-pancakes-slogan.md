### Federal NDP MP Heather McPherson posted a video holding a mug reading "F\*\*\* your fascist pancakes," while Alberta NDP MLA Janis Irwin referenced the slogan in a pancake breakfast post.

Federal NDP MP Heather McPherson and Alberta NDP MLA Janis Irwin are facing criticism after posting social media content referencing the now-viral "fascist pancakes" slogan that emerged from last week's confrontation with Premier Danielle Smith at the K-Days Premier's Breakfast.

McPherson posted a video to Instagram captioned, "Just enjoying my morning coffee," while holding up a mug emblazoned with the phrase "F\*\*\* your fascist pancakes." Joan Jett's *Bad Reputation* plays in the background of the video.

The post quickly drew criticism online, with some accusing the Edmonton Strathcona MP of promoting divisive political rhetoric.
