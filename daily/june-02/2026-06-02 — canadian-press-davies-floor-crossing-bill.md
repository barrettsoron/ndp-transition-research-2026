NDP parliamentary leader Don Davies has introduced a private member's bill — Bill C-278 — that would compel floor crossers to face their voters before switching parties. Per the article's lede, no MP would be permitted to cross the floor without first sitting as an Independent and then contesting and winning a seat under the new party's banner.
