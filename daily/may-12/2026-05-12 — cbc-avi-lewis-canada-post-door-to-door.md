**NDP Leader Avi Lewis says he'll fight to keep door-to-door Canada Post**

May 12 | Duration 9:24

Newly elected federal NDP Leader Avi Lewis was in Winnipeg this week to talk about some of his priorities, including fighting the Liberal government's plan to axe door-to-door mail delivery and his proposal for a national network of public grocery stores. He also talked with CBC Information Radio host Marcy Markusa about how he plans to win over voters who are disillusioned by Prime Minister Mark Carney's government.
