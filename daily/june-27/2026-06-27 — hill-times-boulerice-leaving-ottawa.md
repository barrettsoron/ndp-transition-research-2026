An exit interview with Alexandre Boulerice as he leaves federal politics after 15 years as an NDP MP — and a brief stint as an Independent — to make a provincial run with Québec Solidaire, setting his sights on Quebec's National Assembly. Boulerice was the last sitting MP remaining from the late Jack Layton's 2011 "Orange Wave."
