### Is Carney at the end of his second honeymoon? Plus, polls show a tightening race in Quebec.

**Mark Carney's Liberals continue to hold their lead in the polls over Pierre Poilievre's Conservatives, while Avi Lewis and the New Democrats have yet to breakthrough.**

As the summer slowly approaches, we look ahead into our crystal balls at where the polls might stand later in the year with a game of Buy or Sell — are any of the parties and their leaders over-priced in the polls compared to where they'll be in three months, or are there bargains to be had?

This week on *The Numbers*, we set our expectations for the coming months as we return to a game we last played in September. We also dissect the latest federal polling numbers and take a look at what could be a CAQ comeback in Quebec.
