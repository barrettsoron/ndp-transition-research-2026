The record summarizes Avi Lewis's election as leader of Canada's NDP, won with 56% of the leadership vote on a platform advocating an end to new oil production, a wealth tax, and expanded public services. It notes the party holds six seats in the House of Commons, that Lewis does not hold a seat in Parliament, and that he plans to rebuild the party before seeking election.
