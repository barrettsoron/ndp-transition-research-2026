The federal NDP caucus will gather in Toronto in September to set their priorities for the parliamentary fall session.

Their meeting will be held September 8 to 10.

The focus will be on "tackling the cost of living crisis" and "protecting Canadians from the fallout from the trade war", the party said in a news release today.

NDP Leader Avi Lewis and NDP MPs will also meet with people in Toronto to hear directly about affordability challenges.

Last week, Lewis had a meeting with Prime Minister Mark Carney about affordability and how the Employment Insurance system needs a major overhaul.

## Five NDP MPs

In a Liberal majority House of Commons, the NDP has five seats in the House of Commons, held by Don Davies, Leah Gazan, Gord Johns, Jenny Kwan, and Heather McPherson.

## Quebec seats

Now former NDP MP Alexandre Boulerice resigned his Rosemont—La Petite-Patrie seat a few months ago, to participate in provincial Quebec politics.

A by-election is being held today in the Montreal-area riding of Chicoutimi—Le Fjord where the NDP candidate is Raphaël Émond.

NDP Leader Lewis spent 20 days in Quebec in July, meeting voters and brushing up on his French.
