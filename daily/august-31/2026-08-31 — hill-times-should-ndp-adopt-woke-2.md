Opinion column arguing that the federal NDP under Avi Lewis should not adopt "Woke 2" — an expression used by some left-wing American politicians to signal a more politically moderate tone. Nicholls writes that New Democrats must mainly focus on keeping their supporters energized and motivated, which won't happen if the party suddenly dilutes its progressive principles and values.
