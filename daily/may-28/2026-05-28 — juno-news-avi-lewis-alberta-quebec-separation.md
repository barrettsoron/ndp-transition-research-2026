At an Ottawa news conference on Wednesday, flanked by his caucus, NDP Leader Avi Lewis said he supports Quebec secession while opposing Alberta sovereignty, arguing the two movements are not comparable.

Asked about the apparent inconsistency, Lewis characterized Alberta's movement as "MAGA-aligned" and possibly funded, while describing Quebec's as a "historic" sovereigntist movement. He said there is "no point of comparison" between the two.
