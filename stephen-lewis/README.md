# Stephen Lewis

A research notebook and source index of materials related to Stephen Lewis (1937–2026) — NDP leader, Ontario Leader of the Opposition, Canadian Ambassador to the United Nations, UNICEF Special Envoy for HIV/AIDS in Africa, and father of Avi Lewis.

This subfolder collects publicly available tributes, speeches, interviews, and commentary on his legacy, with particular relevance to the NDP leadership transition of 2026.

It contains two kinds of material:

- **Historical** — speeches and addresses by Stephen Lewis himself (the 2011 Layton eulogy, the 2016 NDP convention speech, the 2018 CUGH keynote).
- **Tributes** — statements and columns published in the days after his death on March 31, 2026, including primary-source statements from Canada's NDP, the Stephen Lewis Foundation, the Confederation of Canadian Unions, and the Toronto and York Region Labour Council, plus longer remembrances from *The Energy Mix* and Ryan Meili's *A Larger Scale*.

Day-of news coverage (CBC obituary, the Carney statement, Seth Klein's farewell column, the resurfaced Avi–Stephen interview, and coverage of the April 26 memorial at TMU) lives in the date-organized `daily/` folders by publication date — primarily `daily/march-31/`, `daily/april-01/`, and `daily/april-26/`. The Stephen Lewis section page on the live site links out to these.

## Scope

Public materials only: published articles, video transcripts, official tributes, public speeches. No private or internal content.

## Format

Files follow the repo naming convention: `YYYY-MM-DD — Title.md`
